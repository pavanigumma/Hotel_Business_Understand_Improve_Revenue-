🏨 Hotel Revenue Strategy & Pricing Optimization Dashboard
Power BI Project Summary
📌 Project Overview
This project enhances the existing hotel analytics model to deliver Revenue Strategy Insights using Power BI. The dashboard analyzes historical revenue, occupancy, pricing, and upsell data to support data-driven decision-making for hotel management.
🎯 Objectives
Identify high-performing seasons and demand peaks
Evaluate room category performance
Assess pricing effectiveness across seasons
Compare revenue by booking source and branch
Identify upsell and upgrade opportunities
Recommend seasonal promotions and pricing tiers
📊 Key KPIs
Total Revenue
Occupancy Rate (%)
ADR (Average Daily Rate)
RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)
Revenue Growth (%)
Upsell Potential (%)
📈 Dashboard Pages
1️⃣ Revenue Strategy Summary
Provides an executive overview of revenue, occupancy trends, ADR, and RevPAR to identify high-performing branches and peak seasons.
2️⃣ Pricing Tier Recommendations
Analyzes ADR and occupancy trends to suggest premium, standard, or promotional pricing strategies based on seasonal performance.
3️⃣ Upsell Opportunity Analysis
Evaluates additional revenue from services such as spa, dining, and premium upgrades. Identifies segments with high upsell potential.
4️⃣ Seasonal Promotion Insights
Highlights low-demand periods and recommends targeted promotional strategies to improve occupancy and revenue.
🎛 Interactive Features
The dashboard includes slicers for:
Hotel Branch / Location
Room Type
Booking Source
Date (Month / Year)
💡 Business Impact
This dashboard converts historical booking data into actionable revenue strategy insights. It enables hotel management to optimize pricing, maximize occupancy, increase upsell revenue, and improve overall profitability through data-driven decisions.
